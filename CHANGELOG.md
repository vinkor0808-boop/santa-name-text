## Santa OreSim 1.2.0

- Menu now has two tabs: **General** and **Colors**
- Pick your own colour for diamonds and ancient debris (colour picker, 12 presets, hex input)
- Fill opacity (0–100 %) and line width (1–6 px) sliders
- Toggles for fill, see-through-walls and an animated rainbow mode
- Live 3D box preview; the menu accent colour follows your diamond colour

## Santa OreSim 1.1.0

- New settings menu (glass style) – open it with **O**, `/oresim menu` or the gear button in **Mod Menu**
- Toggles for ESP, diamonds and ancient debris, seed input (numbers or text seeds), radius 16–256 blocks
- Live status (number of boxes), "Recalculate" button
- German and English translations
